01 — About Interpoint

Wholesale solutions for business.

Interpoint d.o.o. is a business-to-business solution provider focused on wholesale supply and project sourcing. We help organisations find suitable products and coordinate sourcing across our specialist categories.

Looking for retail?

We focus on wholesale rather than direct retail. For retail purchases, please contact the relevant retail partner. For medical equipment, our partner Locum Trade can assist. For other categories, contact us and we’ll direct you to the appropriate partner.
